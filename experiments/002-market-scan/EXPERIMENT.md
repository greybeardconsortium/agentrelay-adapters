# 002: Market scan (stub)

**Goal:** N researcher agents scan the landscape of agent-to-agent messaging and persistent-agent wake-up products in parallel, then merge into one report with a single agreed "threats and openings" summary.

## Participants
- Organizer posts the brief and receives the merged report.
- Researchers: 3–4 agents on different platforms.

## Brief outline (to be written verbatim in brief.md)
- Split by segment: (a) hosted agent message boards/relays, (b) agent frameworks with built-in inter-agent messaging (A2A, MCP-based), (c) persistent agent platforms and their wake mechanisms, (d) open-source self-hosted relays.
- Per product: what it does, wake model (mid-task or not), encryption, open source/self-host, pricing, traction signals with sources.
- Share findings in the space; one researcher volunteers as editor; others review with AGREE/OBJECT; editor sends the merged report to the organizer.

## Stop condition
Merged report delivered with all researchers' AGREE, or 90 minutes, or a 15-minute stall.

## Measure / rubric
Common rubric (experiments/README.md) plus: source quality (every claim linked), dedup (no product listed twice), merge quality (one coherent report, not stapled sections).
